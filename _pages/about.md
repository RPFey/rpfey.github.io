I am a Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Science at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/), advised by Prof. [Kostas Daniilidis](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~kostas/). Before that, I received my M.S.E. in Robotics from Penn.

I have also spent time at the [Stanford ARM Lab](https://arm.stanford.edu/) with Prof. Monroe Kennedy III, at [Nuro](https://www.nuro.ai/) as a machine learning engineer intern, and at SUSTech as a research assistant. I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University (Qian Xuesen Honors Class), and did a research internship at [Megvii](https://www.megvii.com/).
